## Findings

- `audit_installed_profile("studio")` verifies the selected lock and installed
  dependency versions. It permits unrelated installed extras and is not a hash
  audit of all installed dependency code. Do not strengthen its claim in prose.
- The selected CLI path builds six downloadable producers: Sorani Qwen ASR
  validator, Community-1, two Qwen visual models, VideoChat3 and TimeLens2.
  `ModelStore.verify_checkpoint` verifies actual files against protected metadata;
  constructed reports are not capabilities. Loaders still need their existing
  held-descriptor checks at consumption. Community-1 metadata is still blocked.
- Canonical Windows ASR must explicitly use a named WSL generation. Probe the
  actual receipt/assets; do not silently choose a local or different distro.
  Champion fingerprint includes configuration, weights and tokenizer; it is
  identity evidence, not independent licence or speech-accuracy evidence.
- `run_live_gate` performs a fresh hash-locked audit, current VEX evaluation and
  immutable success write. The real current scanner refuses expiry, source/lock
  drift and three undispositioned findings. Never replace this with a supplied
  accepted flag or re-date the VEX policy.
- Signed execution documents bind a supervisor-selected deployment digest, but
  do not currently compare their route/confidentiality to `ProducerSelection`.
  A stable deployment descriptor must exclude its own owner document/signature
  and fresh audit timestamp. Job execution identity separately includes the raw
  signed proof/trust digests. This avoids a signature cycle and unstable replay IDs.
- Provider credentials and editorial transport are not needed to check route
  parity. Actual Vertex constructor validates project/location without acquiring
  ADC; `_url` is the authoritative shared route. The raw signed proof verifier
  must still run on every consumption, not accept a fabricated receipt.
