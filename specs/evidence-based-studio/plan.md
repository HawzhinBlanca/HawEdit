## Frozen Proposed Release Thresholds

- Creator study: at least 11/12 native-Sorani creators finish the whole workflow without
  coaching or CLI flags, zero critical failures.
- Accessibility: all applicable WCAG 2.2 AA, manual keyboard/NVDA, complete workflow.
- Reliability: at least 99/100 supported jobs intended result; report all failures and
  uncertainty; invalid-input refusal separate; zero unbounded hangs/duplicate publication.
- Editorial: at least 95% first-pass publishable, episode-cluster-aware 95% lower bound
  at least 90%; include refusals/no-clip episodes; zero critical fidelity/meaning defects.
- Blinded professional Kurdish-editor comparison: episode-cluster-aware 95% preference
  lower bound above 50%; report ties/dialect slices; no underpowered superiority claim.
- Every publication: current reconciliation, hash/caption/timing invariants, authenticated
  reviewer and identity-labelled speaker evidence. Face presence is not speaker accuracy.
- Recorded hardware: first reviewable clip from 20-minute source in less than 20 minutes;
  cold/warm p50/p95, VRAM, cost and throughput, no unmeasured performance claim.
- Sustained acceptance: 30 days representative use, fresh independent holdout at least
  200 proposals, owner and native editor sign-off, current evidence, no P0/P1.

Development: 200-500 commissioned candidates (§8.2). Final holdout: at least 200 proposals
across at least 20 episodes, disjoint episode/speaker/media; select before running, seal
unblinding, randomize, independent reviewers/adjudication, record correction time.
Episodes are clusters, reviewers repeated observations. Freeze thresholds before evaluation
and do not lower them after seeing results. Run licensed §8.1 dialect/condition references,
report CER/entities/code-switch/alignment/DER and failures separately. Model changes need ADR.
