# Specification — `human-grade-director-v2` (EARS Acceptance Criteria)

## Audio Mastering & Frame-Accurate Cuts (D-360)
- **AC-1 (Zero-double-cut `per_shot_boxes`):** WHEN `crop_filter()` builds multi-shot `per_shot_boxes` expressions (`y_center_expr` and `sendcmd` `w/h` commands), THE renderer SHALL align shot cut switch timestamps `-2 ms` early matching `_interpolated()` so `x`, `y`, `w`, and `h` transition on the exact same video frame even under fractional `-ss` timebase rounding.
- **AC-2 (Broadcast speech mastering):** WHEN `SPEECH_CHAIN_FILTERS` is applied in `measure_audio_loudness()` and `audio_filter()`, THE audio chain SHALL include transparent speech compression and lookahead peak limiting (`acompressor` + `alimiter`) before `loudnorm` so high-crest-factor audio (`+1.5 dBTP` peaks) converges linearly to `-14.0 ± 0.5 LUFS` and `<= -1.0 dBTP`.
- **AC-3 (Content-aware emphasis zoom):** WHEN `content_aware_push_schedule()` is given shot spans, sentence audio metrics (`SentenceAudioMetrics`), and an optional `payoff_at_ms`, THE scheduler SHALL assign stronger eased push-ins (`up to 1.12x`) to high-excitement/payoff spans and calm holds or subtle pushes (`<= 1.04x`) to low-energy spans, while avoiding hard cuts.

## Safe-Zone Camera Director & Cutaway Framing (D-361)
- **AC-4 (Edge-danger fast follow):** WHEN a tracked speaker leans past an explicit edge-danger threshold (`edge_guard_px`) in `stabilize()`, THE stabilizer SHALL initiate a smooth follow pan faster than the normal wobble settle window (`edge_settle_ms < settle_ms`) while remaining 100% tripod-locked inside `dead_zone_px` with zero jitter.
- **AC-5 (Per-shot zero-face cutaway framing):** WHEN `OpenCvFaceTracker.track()` encounters a camera shot inside a multi-shot clip with zero face detections, THE tracker SHALL frame that shot using motion saliency (`detect_motion_focus_points`) instead of blindly holding the prior shot's speaker `x`.

## Face-Aware Hook Card, Zero-Orphan Captions & Acoustic Pop (D-362)
- **AC-6 (Face-aware 2-line hook card, golden-safe):** WHEN `build_ass()` renders `title_ckb` with `face_intervals` present, THE captioner SHALL wrap `title_ckb` up to `30` chars per line (sleek 2-line card) and relocate the hook card off the speaker's face when frame-0 face geometry overlaps the top band, while preserving byte-identical output when `face_intervals is None` (`GOLDEN_HOOK_CARD`).
- **AC-7 (Zero-orphan chunking & Sorani digit normalization):** WHEN `merge_short_orphans=True` is passed to `build_ass()`, THE captioner SHALL merge short trailing 1-word orphans (`<= 4` chars, gap `<= 250 ms`) into the preceding chunk when width fits; AND `caption_polish.normalize_sorani_digits()` SHALL convert isolated Western/Arabic-Indic numerals (`0`–`10`) into Sorani words (`سفر`..`دە`) in display captions without mutating `raw.json` or CTC timestamps.
- **AC-8 (Acoustic-energy karaoke emphasis):** WHEN `excited_intervals_ms` is passed to `build_ass()`, THE captioner SHALL apply a subtle scale micro-pop (`\fscx107\fscy107` -> `\fscx100\fscy100`) only to active karaoke words falling inside acoustically excited intervals.

## Pipeline Integration & Multi-Genre Verification (D-363)
- **AC-9 (Pipeline wiring):** WHEN `run_pipeline()` renders episode review reels, THE pipeline SHALL enable two-pass linear `loudnorm` + `SPEECH_CHAIN_FILTERS`, pass real `audio_metrics` + `verdict.payoff_at_ms` into `content_aware_push_schedule()`, pass high-excitement intervals + `merge_short_orphans=True` into `build_ass()`, and pass `edge_guard_px` into `stabilize()`.
- **AC-10 (Real multi-genre visual verification):** WHEN real production runs execute on both `ai` (`RO1PmSoqmCM`) and `cooking-30m` (`k_jxP903t5w`), THE delivered reels SHALL pass `BroadcastScorecard` with `0` double cuts, linear `-14.0 ± 0.5 LUFS` / `<= -1.0 dBTP`, visually verified non-overlapping hook cards, and zero half-face edge clips, documented in `evidence/human-grade-director-v2-2026-11-21.md`.
