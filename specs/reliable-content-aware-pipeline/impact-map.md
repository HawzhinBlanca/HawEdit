## Protected surfaces and decisions

- BLUEPRINT is frozen. Contract changes need ADRs citing §3/§4/§5/§8; do not allocate or append ADR IDs in this planning task.
- D-263 controls six-file delivery. D-264 controls the audio conditioning chain; conditional bypass requires an explicit revision. D-265 controls content profiles. D-266 controls trimming. D-269/D-270 control caption treatments. D-271 controls independent Path B. D-A3 controls DBOS granularity; D-A6 and later proposal ADRs constrain agent authority. D-272 rejects speculative scale work.
- `scripts/verify.sh`, gate code/count floor, `.github/**`, goldens and fixtures are enforcement surfaces. Create `.codystem-allow-self-edit` before approved changes there; never manually modify the count floor or `.gate` output.
- Model/secret/venv/build restrictions remain in force. A future dependency/model change is its own measured, licensed and approved decision, not part of a generic “modernization” task.
- Vault notes append provenance and contradictions; they do not supersede the repository's approval or CI rules.

## New design elements are proposals

Review-candidate state, effective-config contract, protected-context references and extended timeline mapping are proposed extensions to the existing modules, not asserted existing classes or signatures. Choose exact symbols only after semantic grounding during the approved task. Prefer extending existing data structures over creating another framework or parallel serving path.
